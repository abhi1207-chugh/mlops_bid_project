Rule 176 Buy-Back Offer
When it is decided with the approval
of the competent authority to replace
an existing old item(s) with a new
and better version, the department
may trade the existing old item while
purchasing the new one. For this
purpose, a suitable clause is to be
incorporated
in

document so that the prospective
and interested bidders formulate
their bids accordingly. Depending on
the value and condition of the old

this regard suitably incorporated in
the bidding document. Further,
suitable provision should also be
kept in the bidding document to
enable the purchaser either to trade
or not to trade the item while
